## Moka Pot Requirement

Moka pot coffee needs a fine-to-medium-fine grind: finer than normal drip coffee, but generally not as powdery as true machine espresso. The safest grocery-store filters are:

- `moka` ground, when available.
- `espresso style` / `espresso ground`, when it is a supermarket pre-ground product and not ultra-fine specialty espresso powder.
- Whole bean only if a grinder is available and can grind medium-fine.

Bialetti's own support page says to loosely fill the funnel with espresso-ground coffee and not press it down. Lavazza's official product page for Espresso Italiano Classico Ground lists `Moka` as a supported preparation method.

Sources:
- Bialetti Moka Express instructions: https://bialetti-cookware.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/5416235346322-How-to-use-the-Moka-Express
- Lavazza Espresso Italiano Ground Coffee: https://www.lavazzausa.com/en/roast-and-ground-coffee/espresso.3036

## User Correction Learned

The user's correction was: "do two bags of Bustelo because that is too expensive and it is the same amount."

Decision rule update:

- An upgrade must provide a clearly better experience, not just a better brand name.
- For daily coffee, quantity matters because running out creates friction.
- Paying about the same order cost for roughly half the coffee is a failed cart decision.
- The preferred strategy is a cheap reliable default plus optional experiments only when the experiment does not distort the grocery budget.

## Why Not illy This Time
