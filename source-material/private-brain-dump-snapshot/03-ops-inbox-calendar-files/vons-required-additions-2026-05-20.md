## Protein Replacement Update

Follow-up live research found better ROI alternatives to the Just Bare slot:

- Best low-friction replacement: Tyson Grilled & Ready Diced Oven Roasted Chicken Breast or Tyson Frozen Grilled Chicken Breast Strips, each $9.99 / 22 oz (~$7.27/lb). This preserves the fully cooked frozen chicken use case while beating Just Bare at ~$9.23-$9.33/lb.
- Cheapest microwave chicken: Foster Farms Classic Chicken Patties, $6.99 / 28 oz (~$3.99/lb), or Tyson Fully Cooked Chicken Patties, $7.99 / 23 oz (~$5.56/lb). These are cheaper but more processed and less lean.
- Best protein ROI if cooking is available: Signature Select/Farms frozen boneless skinless chicken breasts or tenderloins, $9.99 / 40 oz (~$4.00/lb).
- Beef does not beat chicken for this slot unless the user specifically wants beef. Best beef compromise was Signature SELECT 91% Lean 9% Fat Ground Beef, $14.99 / 40 oz (~$6.00/lb), but it requires cooking.
